My Story

From Ratta to Kabarak — building my way forward

I was born to my wonderful parents, John Olao & Grace Anyango, and raised as a proud grandson of Olao & Omollo Masella, whose stories and values still guide me today.

My journey started at Ratta Primary & Secondary School, where I developed a curiosity for how things work. That curiosity pulled me towards computer science at Kabarak University, where I earned my Bachelor of Computer Science and wrote my first real programs.

Today I build web applications and database systems with Django, Python, JavaScript and SQL. What drives me is simple: turn a real problem into a clean solution that someone can actually use. Outside of code you will find me behind a camera, on the football pitch, or mentoring young developers who are just starting out.
